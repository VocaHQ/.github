# Code of conduct

We want Voca spaces to be welcoming and useful for contributors and users. Be respectful, assume good intent, and discuss ideas and code without personal attacks. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, and sharing someone else's private information are not acceptable.

This applies to Voca repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or block accounts when needed to protect participants. They will consider the context and impact of a report before acting.

Report conduct concerns privately to [hello@vocahq.com](mailto:hello@vocahq.com). Include links or screenshots and enough context to investigate. We will handle reports as privately as practical and avoid sharing a reporter's identity without a reason to do so. Security vulnerabilities have a separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
