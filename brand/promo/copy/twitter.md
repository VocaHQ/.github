## Family thread

1. Voca is speech-to-text on the machines you already own. No Voca account. On-device after a model download. [card-family-wide.png]

2. VocaLinux is available now. X11 or Wayland. Current release v0.16.0. vocalinux.com [card-linux-square.png]

3. VocaMac is beta v0.9.0 on macOS 14+ Apple Silicon. Homebrew or a DMG. vocamac.com [card-mac-square.png]

4. VocaWin is beta. Unsigned tester builds are on GitHub. SmartScreen / unknown publisher is expected. vocawin.com [card-win-square.png]

5. VocaPhone: Android 13+ public beta. iOS 17+ is a source build if you have a Mac and a signing team. vocaphone.vocahq.com [card-phone-square.png]

6. VocaGateway is optional self-hosted compute. Audio goes to a host you run. That is not on-device. github.com/VocaHQ/vocagateway [card-gateway-square.png]

7. We do not claim 100% offline. The first model download uses the network. After that, dictation stays on that machine unless you aim it at a gateway.

8. Family home is vocahq.com. Source and releases: github.com/VocaHQ
