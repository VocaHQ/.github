01

the manifesto

small tools.
clear boundaries.

Voca is a family of speech-to-text tools for machines you already own. Hold a hotkey or tap the phone control. Text lands where you type. The boundary stays visible.

01

On-device first.

After the model download, audio and the speech-to-text model stay on the phone or computer.

02

Free and open source.

The actual license is named per project. Usually AGPL-3.0. VocaWin is AGPL-3.0-or-later.

03

Text lands where you type.

Hold a hotkey or tap the phone control. Words appear at the cursor when the platform allows it.

04

No required account.

There is no Voca login. No hosted speech cloud to sign up for.

05

Honest status.

Available, beta, alpha, early. We say what ships, and we do not invent a screenshot.

06

Built in public.

Source and releases live on github.com/VocaHQ. Status follows PRODUCT.md.

02

path / a · on this device

on this
device.

After the model download, audio and the speech-to-text model stay on the phone or computer.

Download and update still use the network. Do not call this offline in the absolute sense.

A gateway is not required for this path.

A · audio stays on the machine you spoke into

path / b · your vocagateway

or your
gateway.

Configured audio leaves the client for the self-hosted machine you selected.

Trusted LAN, encrypted private network, or HTTPS. The gateway runs a local speech engine and returns the transcript.

Never call this on-device. VocaGateway is optional shared compute, not a client.

B · audio travels to hardware you host · never on-device
