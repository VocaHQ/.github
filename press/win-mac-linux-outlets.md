## How to use this list

1. **A first.** Personalized tip, not a press-release blast. One platform per email (Vocalinux / VocaWin / VocaMac).
2. Lead with the hook editors actually cover: **on-device / no cloud / AGPL / system-wide dictation / privacy vs Whisper-in-the-cloud.**
3. Include a short demo URL, GitHub, and one screenshot. No "honest/genuine" AI filler (TidBITS called this out in July 2026).
4. Skip rows marked **DEAD**, **listing-only**, or **LATER**.
5. Every **A/B** row below has a verified contact **or** is explicitly **UNVERIFIED**.
