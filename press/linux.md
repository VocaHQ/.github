## How to use this list

Personalized tip, not a press-release blast. One platform per email. Lead with on-device / no cloud / AGPL / system-wide dictation. Include a demo URL, GitHub, and one screenshot. Skip UNVERIFIED, DEAD, listing-only, and LATER rows. Do not invent contacts.
